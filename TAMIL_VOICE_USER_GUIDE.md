# How to Enable Tamil Voice - Simple Instructions

**This guide is for making Tamil voice work on the complaint form.**

---

## 🎯 What You Need to Do

The system is ready for Tamil voice, but **your device/browser needs Tamil language installed**.

---

## 📱 For Android Phone/Tablet Users

### Step 1: Open Settings
- Swipe down from top → Click ⚙️ **Settings**
- Or find **Settings** in your app drawer

### Step 2: Find Language Settings
- Tap **Language** or **Languages & input**
- Look for **Regional formats** or **Language preferences**

### Step 3: Add Tamil
- Tap **Add language** or **+**
- Search for **Tamil** (தமிழ்)
- Tap **Tamil** to add it

### Step 4: Restart Browser
- Close the complaint form browser
- Open it again
- Try the voice feature

✅ **Tamil voice should now work!**

---

## 💻 For Windows 10 / Windows 11 Computer

### Step 1: Open Settings
- Press **Windows key + I** 
- Or click **Start** → **Settings** ⚙️

### Step 2: Go to Language Settings
- Click **Time & Language**
- Click **Language** (left side)

### Step 3: Add Tamil Language
- Click **+ Add a language**
- Search: **Tamil** or **தமிழ்**
- Click **Tamil** result
- Click **Next**
- Click **Install**
- Wait for download (2-5 minutes)

### Step 4: Restart Computer
- Restart your Windows computer
- Open complaint form browser again

✅ **Tamil voice should now work!**

---

## 🍎 For Mac (Apple Computer)

### Step 1: Open System Preferences
- Click **Apple menu** (top-left)
- Click **System Preferences**

### Step 2: Find Language Settings
- Click **Language & Region**

### Step 3: Add Tamil
- Click **+** (bottom-left)
- Search for **Tamil**
- Click **Tamil**
- Click **Add**

### Step 4: Restart Computer
- Restart your Mac
- Open complaint form browser again

✅ **Tamil voice should now work!**

---

## 📱 For iPhone / iPad

### Step 1: Open Settings
- Tap **Settings** ⚙️

### Step 2: Find Accessibility
- Tap **Accessibility**
- Tap **Speech**

### Step 3: Change Language to Tamil
- Tap **Language**
- Search for **Tamil** or **தமிழ்**
- Tap **Tamil**

### Step 4: Download Tamil Voice
- If prompted to download, tap **Download**
- Wait for it to finish

### Step 5: Restart Safari
- Close Safari browser completely
- Open it again
- Go to complaint form

✅ **Tamil voice should now work!**

---

## 🔍 How to Check If Tamil Voice Works

### Quick Test:
1. Open the complaint form
2. Select **Tamil language** from dropdown
3. Click **microphone 🎤** button
4. **Listen** for the voice prompt

**You should hear:** Tamil voice asking for input  
**If you don't hear anything:** Follow the installation steps above

---

## ❓ Troubleshooting

### Problem: I installed Tamil but still no voice

**Solution:**
1. **Fully close the browser** (not just minimize)
2. **Restart your device** completely
3. Open complaint form again
4. Try microphone button

### Problem: I hear English voice instead of Tamil

**Good news!** This means the system is working. The voice will still help guide you through the form, and you can speak in Tamil.

### Problem: Browser shows "Microphone access denied"

**Solution:**
1. Click the microphone icon in address bar
2. Select **Allow** for microphone
3. Try again

### Problem: No sound at all

**Solution:**
1. Check device volume is not muted
2. Check browser sound settings not muted
3. Make sure speakers/headphones are connected
4. Restart the browser
